# NotebookLM Usage Notes Policy

NotebookLM notes are useful for candidate issue maps, workflow hints, and practical use/do-not-use rules. They are not legal authority.

## Allowed Uses

- Suggest document intent and workflow stage.
- Suggest clause usage and blockers.
- Identify missing facts and evidence tasks.
- Link notes to templates/clauses for lawyer review.

## Not Allowed

- Supporting final legal propositions without public authority.
- Replacing public source cards.
- Overriding procedural gates.
- Publishing private notes or extracted private text.

## Status Labels

NotebookLM-derived records use INTERNAL_USAGE_NOTE. If converted into a route or rule by AI, the proposed rule remains AI_SUGGESTED until lawyer approval or firm SOP review.
