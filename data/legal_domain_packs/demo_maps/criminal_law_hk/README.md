## Coverage

1. Fundamentals (actus reus, mens rea, causation, strict liability)
2. Offences against the person (murder, manslaughter, assault, sexual offences)
3. Property and dishonesty (theft, fraud, criminal damage)
4. Bribery and corruption (POBO ss.3, 4, 5, 9, 10, misconduct in public office)
5. Anti-money laundering (OSCO s.25, Cap 615 regulatory framework)
6. Complicity and inchoate offences (joint enterprise, conspiracy, attempt)
7. General defences (insanity, automatism, intoxication, self-defence, duress)
8. Statutes and ordinances index

## Key HK divergences from English law

- Recklessness: subjective test (Sin Kam Wah v HKSAR) — Caldwell overruled
- Joint enterprise: Chan Wing Siu retained (HKSAR v Chan Kam Shing) — Jogee rejected
- Strict liability: five-tier framework (Kulemesin v HKSAR) — not binary
- Oblique intent: Woollin adopted (HKSAR v Li Defan)
- Dishonesty: HK position on Ivey post-Lam Hin Fai pending verification
